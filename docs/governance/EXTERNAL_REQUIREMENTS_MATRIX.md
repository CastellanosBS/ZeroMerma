# ZeroMerma External Requirements Matrix

## Purpose and authority

This document is the canonical ZM-FIN-007 traceability package. It maps approved external requirements and business policies to data, modules, controls, evidence, owners, implementation tasks, and gates. It defines what must be built and verified; it does not claim that ZeroMerma is fiscally, privacy, PCI, backup, or BBVA compliant.

Authority order used for this package:

1. current repository code;
2. DEC-16 and the other approved decisions in `docs/DECISIONES_ZERO_MERMA.md`;
3. the 126-task plan in `docs/PLAN_MAESTRO_FINALIZACION_ZERO_MERMA.md`;
4. versioned supporting documentation;
5. historical audit findings.

The legal and provider sources listed below are the evidence already recorded by DEC-16. They were not reinterpreted or refreshed in ZM-FIN-007. Entity-specific fiscal configuration and the final BBVA/PCI data flow remain external validations.

## Privacy and ARCO boundary

The initial customer model remains operation-specific and branch-local; no persistent global Customer profile is required. A versioned notice must record its effective version and presentation surface without inventing consent. Future implementation must distinguish `NOTICE_PRESENTED`, `CONSENT_NOT_REQUIRED`, `CONSENT_REQUIRED`, `CONSENT_GRANTED`, and `CONSENT_REVOKED` only when applicable.

The ARCO workflow requires intake, verified subject/representative, controller, right/request scope, `received_at`, target decision in 20 days, one justified extension, decision/reason, target execution within 15 days after approval, execution evidence, legal hold, and audit. This is approved policy but has no sufficient implementation task in the current Plan.

## Third-party and multi-tenant boundary

For a third-party bakery, the entity determining purposes is the controller and ZeroMerma acts as processor when operating under instructions. Before onboarding, evidence must cover instructions, purpose/scope, confidentiality/security, incidents, subprocessors/transfers, ARCO assistance, return/deletion at termination, and retention. Branch/tenant scope is mandatory. Identifiable cross-tenant secondary use is prohibited by default; transversal analytics must be aggregated or disassociated unless later validly authorized.
